# Kurzgesagt playbook

Measured from 20 popular science-explainer Shorts, frame by frame (study only; every word, character and scene is ours).

## Pace
- Calm narrator at **2.97 words a second**. The first word lands at 0.2 s, and there is no dead air.
- About **12 shot changes** in 60 s. The median shot is **4.7 s**.
- 62 % of frames have the camera moving.
- About half of all changes are hard cuts to a new composition. The rest zoom through an object into the next scene, iris open, wipe, or dissolve (once or twice).

## Look
- **A whole illustrated world in every shot**, never a card on a plain background. It is drawn in 3–5 planes that slide at about 1 : 1.3 : 2 speed:
  - a far gradient sky or sea with soft blobs and stars;
  - mid props;
  - the hero plane;
  - a crisp, dark near layer that slides fastest.
- **One big hero** at 30–60 % of the frame width, with a glow behind it. 8–25 objects per frame in all; about 140 flat shapes in a typical frame.
- **Flat lighting with no outlines.** Light comes from the upper left. Each shape gets a thin bright rim on the light side, a darker crescent on the far side, and a soft glow around anything that shines.
- **Saturated gradient grounds** that jump to a complementary pair each scene: #f6b50e→#3e1060, #3db8f5→#2d1f6b. Accents are magenta #ff1f9d, sun yellow and pill purple. Luma p50 is about 0.34; corners have a soft vignette.

## Life
Nothing is ever still:
- characters blink every 2–3 s, bob 3–6 px and look around;
- stars twinkle, particles drift, glows pulse;
- fish, birds and cells cross the background;
- props pop in with an overshoot over 6–10 frames.

Something pops or changes every 20–30 frames.

## Characters
- Round, simple bodies with big white oval eyes and a highlight.
- No nose; a small mouth only when it needs one. White gloves on hands.
- Everyday things become characters: a polyp, the sun, a cell, an egg.
- They react on the words: worried, then happy.
- They act with the whole body on the words: hop, squash, wave, nod, shake (`act`). A face change alone isn't acting.
- Never stack two faces on top of each other.

## Camera
- Mix the moves: slow push-ins on at most 6 in 10 shots; the rest hold still, pan, follow or pull out. Punch in fast on the key word.
- A real transition (zoom-through, iris, wipe, dissolve) on about half the scene changes, on a phrase boundary.
- The plain gradient stage at most twice; the colour family changes every 2–3 shots.

## Text
- Title in the first 2–3 s: white, with one hot-pink word.
- At most one purple pill and one yellow tag per scene.
- Captions: white with a dark outline, 2–3 words per card, at 78–87 % of the frame height.

## Sound
- −14 LUFS; the voice is compressed and forward.
- Soft synth pops on pop-ins, whooshes on zoom-throughs.
- No music bed and no ambience.
