# Team playbook

## Look
Polished anime 2D. Bold ink outlines, cel shading with a single hard-edged shadow tone, dramatic dusk skies with light rays and long cloud streaks, halftone corners, speed lines and impact frames. Characters are big (35 to 55 percent of frame height) and stand on a ground plane that runs to the bottom edge, with a skyline or stands behind, a light pole mid-ground and weeds in front. Five parallax layers move under a camera that is never still.

## Palette
Night navy and dusk magenta for the world, one team colour (default orange `#ff6a2b`) on every jersey, chip and headline accent. Cream paper for bubbles and cards, ink `#120c1c` for outlines. Rivals use the opposite hue.

## Type
Archivo Black for headlines, chips and numbers (white with a thick ink stroke and a drop shadow), Plus Jakarta Sans heavy for bubbles and comments. Headlines 2 to 5 words, all caps.

## Series chrome
- DAY N chip top left on the broadcast bar, skewed slab in the team colour.
- Up to two counter chips top right (OG fans, Team fund), counting up.
- Text only in the top zone (about 12 to 27 percent of the height at 9:16). Captions are drawn at y 61 percent: keep faces and text clear of y 57 to 66 percent.

## Motion
Whip-in between scenes, punch-in and shake on impacts, push-in and drift otherwise, whip pans between training beats, slam-in headlines, coins and sparkles on wins. Cel overlay and ghost trails on runs.

## Grounds
Run-down = cracks, weeds, patches, dim lights, sparse crowd. Patched = fewer cracks and one clean patch. Fresh = clean surface, bright lines, full lights. Line markings use real dimensions (NBA half court, FIFA pitch, beach volleyball, ring, NHL rink).

## Sound
Light drum bed, thumps for slams, a crowd swell on wins. No whistles, risers or chimes.

## Hooks
- Start inside the problem ("our home court is a parking lot").
- The crowd of OG fans as the proof of progress.
- End on a two-option question the comments can answer in a few words.

## Series rules
One small real step per episode. Counters only from series.json. Keep the cast, the team colour and the sport stable.
