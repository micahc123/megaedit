# Roblox-style story 3D playbook

## Look
R6-style blocky avatars: a rounded-cylinder head with a drawn decal face, block torso and limbs, flat shirt colours or
painted prints, chunky hair. The chest number is the hook of the genre: big, glowing, green for good, red for bad, and it
changes on screen at the turn. Places are built from the story, Roblox-Studio-looking (smooth plastic, simple props,
baseplate grass, city backdrops), lit like Roblox "Future" lighting: soft shadows, warm interiors, volumetric beams at night.

## Camera
Always moving: a slow push-in or pull on every shot (the reference moves 90% of frames). Medium shots on the subject,
head in the upper third, the chest number readable. Low angles for wins, high angles for the victims, a hard cut on
every clause. Lens blur behind the subject (`fstop` 1.0-2.0).

## Captions
Two short lines in Lilita One, white with a dark outline, placed off the chest number. Key words in colour.

## Sound
Fast, flat narrator (about 4.3 words a second). The kit's coded bed changes mood at the turn (playful → dark). Pops and
hits on reveals. No ambience bed.

## Script formula
1. The rule of this world, one sentence, over a crowd showing it.
2. Green example, red example.
3. One kid wakes up with the extreme number.
4. Three fast wins, three new places.
5. Home: the number flips red and drops to an absurd value.
6. Why, in one line.
7. Who it cost, deadpan.
