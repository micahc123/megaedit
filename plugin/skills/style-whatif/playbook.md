# What if playbook

## Look
Flat vector, no outlines on the world, a thick ink outline on people. The city is drawn in layers that slide at different speeds when the camera moves: sky, far skyline, near skyline, street, and an optional foreground. A soft vignette sits on every shot. The hero is a round-headed figure in one bright coat (the only saturated colour in the frame); passers-by are dull and desaturated so the hero is always the first thing you see.

## The future is a ghost
Anything that has not happened yet is drawn in one see-through colour (cyan by default) with a glow: a ghost copy of a walker a few seconds ahead, a ghost cup mid-fall, a ghost car that cannot stop. When it comes true, the real thing arrives where the ghost was. One ghost colour per episode.

## Palette
Dusk: sky `#3a3356` to `#e49b78`, ink `#15161c`. Day: `#8fb0c6` to `#ead6b6`. Night: `#14162a` to `#2c2a48`. Accent (eyes, counter, big question) amber `#ffb83f`; ghost `#6ee1ff`; danger `#ff5a4a`.

## Type
Archivo Black for tags, the counter and the big question. Inter 700 italic for the lead-in. Captions are small, bold, white with the keyword in amber, at 80 % of the frame height.

## Pacing
About 3.3 words a second. A new shot every 2 to 4 seconds; never hold one picture for a whole long line. Cut on the word that explains the picture ("A faint copy ...": cut as the ghost appears). Camera is always gliding or pushing; add a short shake on every impact.

## Beats that work
- Eyes light up on the power's key word.
- One fact beat on a blueprint grid with two bars (what everyone gets vs what you get).
- A near miss: the ghost shows it, you move first, the real thing flies past where you were.
- The cost: nobody else saw it, so it looks like you did something wrong. Desaturate and blur the background, hero in front.
- A quiet night scene, then warm light creeping in on the last twist.
- End on a question that makes people take a side.

## Colour grade by beat
Warm `rgba(255,140,90,.22)` for the fun, no grade for the day action, grey-blue `rgba(60,90,160,.25)` for the "nobody saw", deep blue `rgba(70,80,200,.28)` for the night.
