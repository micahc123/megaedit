# Paper collage playbook

## Look
A table of magazine clippings, photographed and animated. Every piece has a hand-torn edge (jagged clip path), a hard offset shadow and slight rotation. "Photos" are halftone prints: a tinted torn block, a misregistered colour blob, a duotone icon silhouette and a dot screen. Tape strips hold things down. Characters are die-cut stickers with a white border. A newsprint background of grey text bars sits behind everything.

## Palette
bg `#e9e2d0` (newsprint), ink `#1b1a17`, accent `#c8372d` (printer red), paper `#f7f2e4`, muted `#7b7466`. Add kraft brown `#c9a46c` and tape yellow sparingly. Before images go grey, after images get the accent.

## Type
Fraunces italic (variable weight; only the italic is bundled) for headlines and quotes; Instrument Serif (regular only) for labels, which runs narrow so use it larger. Ransom headlines: each letter on its own tile, mixing both faces and paper, ink and accent tiles, each rotated a few degrees.

## Motion
Things are pasted down: a 1.25 to 1 scale slap, then rest. Marker ellipses and ticks draw themselves. The reveal sheet peels up and away. Everything has a slow handheld sway.

## Camera
Handheld by default. Push-in on the reveal, pan along a map, tilt up a long page. No whips.

## Sound
Paper tears, tape pulls, a stamp thud, a pencil scratch. Sparse low piano or room-tone-free pad at -24 dB. No whistles, risers, chimes or any high held tones.

## Hooks
A found object ("a note in a coat I bought for ten dollars"), a small mystery, or a quiet confession. End on an open question and an invitation to ask for the next page.
