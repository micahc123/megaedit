# Road-to playbook

## Look
A painted toy world with black ink lines, saturated sky, fog into the horizon colour and lens blur on the background. Flat inked cut-out characters stand in it and scale with the camera. The camera glides on every shot, never static. Text is on flat sticker cards inside the safe box; a small dark DAY chip rides top-left.

## Worlds
football (stadium, stands, fence, lockers, ball: yard lines and numbers, grandstands, chain-link fence, locker room, a hero ball; dusky purple and pink), field (mown stripes, painted lines, a goal frame, bleachers, floodlights), gym (wood floor, banner line, hoop, wall bars, lockers), street (houses, corner shop, parked cars, lamps), office (carpet, desks with monitors, glass wall, big window). The series accent colour paints roofs, awnings, banners and seats.

## Pace
About 3.3 words per second, 45 to 55 s. Suspense, not speed: never speed the voice up to fit.

## Sound
ElevenLabs narration, short soft SFX only. No music beds with leads, no risers, no whistles, no ambience.

## Ending
One concrete either/or on the vote card, then "Top comment decides Day N".
