# You pick playbook

## Look
A comic strip that moves. Panels with thick ink borders and hard offset shadows, halftone dots in corners, speech bubbles with tails, spiky burst stars for emphasis. Rooms are drawn with the same thick outline at reduced opacity so the hero pops. Characters use the `ink` skin with reaction faces: worry, cry, smug, dizzy, happy.

## Palette
bg `#fde7d9` (peach), ink `#24161b`, accent `#ff5c7a` (hot pink), paper `#fff6ef`, muted `#b48a8f`. Choice option B uses the accent hue rotated 180 degrees so A and B are always distinct. Loser cards grey out and tilt.

## Type
Lilita One for display, outlined and fat; Bricolage Grotesque for small labels. Words pop in with overshoot. Headlines 1 to 4 words, all caps is fine.

## Motion
Punchy: energy high, overshoot, quick zooms. A 7 percent camera punch lands on the emphasis word. The before and after wipe fires about a third of the way through the scene. Card flips and flashes mark the twist.

## Camera
Punchy push-ins, whip into a choice, shake for panic. Use static for reaction beats so the face reads.

## Sound
Comedic thumps, pops, a soft record scratch, a cartoon boing. Light bouncy bed at -22 dB. No whistles, risers, chimes or any held high tone.

## Hooks
Stakes plus a clock in line one ("a date in ten minutes"). Then speak straight to the viewer and hand them the vote. Payoff must beat the setup, and the ending hands over to the next pick.
