# Fern documentary 3D playbook

## Look
A reconstruction, not a cartoon: realistic dark 3D sets (worn wood, plaster, wet asphalt, rain) and the machines and
objects of the story, one practical light per set and everything else falling into black. People are rare: faceless
mannequins in dark clothes, seen from behind or in shadow; the place, the machine and the evidence carry the story. Shallow focus, a soft
bloom on the lights, a cool desaturated grade with a single accent red (the tag, the route, the ring), a vignette and
film grain. Real places read from their props and light, not from detail.

## Camera
Slow, deliberate and close. Few cuts: 3D holds 3.5-7 s, inserts 2.5-4 s, so one shot carries several caption lines.
Most 3D shots are tight on a part of the thing at the centre of the story (a jet: `jet.tail`, `jet.engine`, `jet.gear`,
`jet.stairs` with `cu`/`ecu`), lit from one side so the rest of the frame falls into black, with a slow push or drift
(`amt` 0.12-0.22: the camera always creeps). One wide shot at most per place; `ins` and `top` inserts for the evidence.

## Documents and maps
Every story has its paper: a note, a report, a ticket, a newspaper. It slides into a shallow-focus close-up and the
key phrase gets a yellow highlighter sweep as the voice says it, labelled RECONSTRUCTION. Maps are dark real terrain
with a red route drawing on and a pulsing ring where the trail ends, a label with the date or the estimate.

## Captions and text
White caps captions in Archivo Black with a black edge, one to three words at a time, low-middle of the frame. A small
red tag at the top (`THE CASE · YEAR`). One date-and-place stamp lower left, on the opening shot only (maps carry their own labels). The end
card: the case name and one line.

## Sound
A calm, low narrator (about 2.4 words a second), present tense, warm and dark. Under it a coded score that never drops
out: a deep drone, a low wash and a slow pulse, lightly ducked under the voice and swelling at the end. Foley only where the picture calls for it: rain on rain shots, paper on
documents, a low whoosh on maps, a thump on the big cut. No ambience bed.

## Script formula
1. Date + place + an action, present tense.
2. Who, what's at stake, one number.
3. The act, step by step, with the document.
4. The escape or turn, on the map.
5. Years later: what was found.
6. The open question. End card.
