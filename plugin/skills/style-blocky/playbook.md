# Block World 3D playbook

## Look
Chunky figures built from rounded boxes: a cube head a third of the body tall, a drawn face (thick brows, pill eyes with a white glint, a mouth with fourteen moods), mitten hands, big dark shoes, hair and hats made of blocks. They are real 3D, so they turn three-quarters to the camera, swing their arms and cast a soft blob shadow. Two-tone toon shading, a thin ink line on every edge, lens blur behind the subject. Rooms come from the shared room kit and change every beat.

## Camera
Medium shots first, the head a little above the middle, with the top fifth of the frame kept clear for the caption and the tag. Push-in by default; travel between shots; a shake or hard cut on the twist.

## Head tags
White display type with a dark outline, anchored over the head and scaled with distance. A changing number rolls for about half a second and pops. One tag per character.

## Captions
Up to five words on one or two lines, Lilita One, white with a dark outline, at 17% of the frame height.

## Motion
Arms act on the side the character faces. Talking nods the head and cycles the mouth every four frames; blinks every 40 to 90 frames. Walks and runs swing arms and legs and turn the body side-on.

## Sound
Voice fast and dry (about 4.2 words a second). Pops and bonks on tag changes and reactions. Music light or none. No risers, no whistles, no ambience bed.

## Script formula
1. The rule of this world, one sentence.
2. What normal looks like (two quick examples, tags showing).
3. One kid wakes up different.
4. Three fast wins, each in a new room.
5. The door opens: the catch.
6. Why, in one line.
7. Where they are now, deadpan.
