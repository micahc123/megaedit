# Paper 2D playbook

## Look
A craft table photographed from above and flattened. Every element is a piece of paper: cardstock cards with a thin ink outline and a soft offset shadow, torn tape on corners, pencil lines. Characters are paper dolls with the `ink` skin. Nothing glows, nothing is glossy.

## Palette
bg `#efe6d6` (oat), ink `#2b2622`, accent `#d9653b` (terracotta), paper `#fbf6ec`, muted warm grey. One accent per scene; override with `visual.accent` only for a deliberate colour beat. Never pure black or white.

## Type
Friendly rounded display for headlines (max 6 words), humanist sans for labels. Headlines sit on paper strips, never float on the background. Fit to the content box; no text outside it.

## Motion
Gentle springs, energy about .5. Pieces slide in like they are being placed, settle with a tiny rotation. Lines draw themselves. Numbers count up. No hard flashes.

## Camera
Slow push-in on the hero beat, shake only for a mistake, pan along a process. Handheld wobble is mild.

## Sound
Soft paper rustle, pop on placement, light tick on counters. Quiet warm music at -22 dB. No whistles, risers, chimes or high pitched tones.

## Hooks
A small personal failure ("I folded forty planes"), a gentle curiosity ("the trick is only three folds"), or a before and after promise. First line must land inside 3 seconds.
