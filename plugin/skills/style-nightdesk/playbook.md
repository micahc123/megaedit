# Night desk playbook

The measured format of animated "builder and AI" desk skits: 6 reference reels, every frame measured at 30 fps. Our characters, set and wording are original. Only the grammar and the numbers carry over.

## Numbers to hit
| | Reference median | Ours |
|---|---|---|
| Length | 49 s (36–60) | 35–50 s |
| Words / s | 2.2 | 2.2–2.5 |
| First word | 0.39 s | < 0.4 s |
| Cuts | 17.5 | one per line, 12–16 |
| Median shot | 2.2 s (1.1–3.9) | 2–3.5 s |
| Moving shots | 34 % (pull-outs > push-ins) | default move rotation |
| Flashes, shakes, dissolves | 0 | 0 |
| Loudness | −14.9 LUFS | −14 to −15 |

## Look
- **Room:** a dark room at night. A big window shows a dusk gradient (violet → magenta → apricot), or navy night, or rain. Blocky towers have warm and soft-cyan lit windows.
- **Light:** a warm desk-lamp key on the faces, a lilac rim from the window, and a heavy vignette.
- **Depth:** shallow depth of field. In close-ups the window is pure bokeh.
- **Maker:** a chibi with a head about half her seated height, bead eyes, chunky rounded-square glasses, a blunt bob with a gold clip, and an oversized hoodie.
- **Sidekick:** a pebble bot about mug-sized, with a dark visor, glowing glyph eyes and a sprout antenna.
- **Palette:** bg `#241834`, ink `#16121c`, accent `#f0b23a` (the antenna bulb and the pen), paper `#fff6ea`. Saturation stays under 75 %.

## Shot grammar
- About 80 % of shots are close-ups on the speaker (maker or sidekick).
- One `two` shot for the turn.
- One `screen` for the sponsor slot, plus optionally one more for a chat, error or app screen.
- The camera holds, pulls out slowly or pushes in slowly. No fast moves.

## Sound
Dry voices only. Room tone is optional, and there is no music bed. The reference has none, which keeps the jokes clean.

## Why brands pay for this format
- **The ad is the punchline.** The product arrives as the fix, and the sidekick's overreaction shows it working.
- **The reach is cheap.** One set and two characters means a new episode is just a script, so the account can post daily.
- **The funnel stacks:**
  - the sponsor slot in the video;
  - "made with X" plus a code in the caption;
  - "comment LINK" sends a DM with tracked links;
  - a giveaway with brand-prize slots.

## 10 original episode ideas (sponsor slot noted)
1. **"Pip, remind me to drink water."** Pip can't act when the tab is closed. Slot: a reminder or notification app. *(the example)*
2. **"Pip, read this PDF to me while I cook."** Pip only reads text, and "Ingredient one: page one." Slot: a text-to-voice app. Button: Pip starts doing accents.
3. **"Pip, what did I spend on coffee this month?"** "I can't see your bank. I can see your mug. It's empty." Slot: a budgeting tool with an MCP. Button: Pip puts the mug on a budget.
4. **"Pip, join my 10 a.m. call."** "I don't have a face." Slot: an AI note-taker. Pip becomes the meeting's favourite attendee.
5. **"Pip, post this for me."** Pip writes 40 drafts and posts none. Slot: a scheduler. Button: Pip schedules a post about being scheduled.
6. **"Pip, fix my website on my phone."** "I live on this desk." Slot: a mobile coding app. Pip ships from the bus.
7. **"Pip, tell me if the build breaks."** "I'll know when you scream." Slot: a monitoring or uptime tool. Pip gets anxious about every ping.
8. **"Pip, make me a logo."** Pip describes a logo in 300 words. Slot: an image or design tool. Pip makes 90 logos of itself.
9. **"Pip, remember my friend's birthday."** "I forget everything when you close me." Slot: a memory or notes app. Pip remembers everything, including that you forgot last year.
10. **"Pip, explain webhooks like I'm five."** Non-sponsored teaching episode: a `screen` with a doorbell diagram. Button: "Some doorbells never ring."

## Writing checklist
- The hook is "<Name>, <normal thing>." spoken by the maker in the first 0.4 s.
- Every line is 2–12 words, and every line is spoken by the maker or the sidekick.
- There is one `two` shot (the turn), and at most one sponsor slot, which has a disclosure.
- The product shows up through the sidekick's behaviour, not a feature list.
- The button calls back to the hook.
- Run `megaedit check`. It flags a sponsor slot that never says the brand, a second sponsor slot, and long lines.
