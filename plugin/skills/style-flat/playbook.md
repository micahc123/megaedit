# Flat vector playbook

## Look
Geometry first: circles, rings, rounded rectangles, one flat shade each. Characters use the `flat` skin on colour discs with a soft body shade and a ground ellipse. Backgrounds are tinted shapes drifting slowly. No grain, no outlines heavier than 2 px, no photos.

## Palette
bg `#f3efe7`, ink `#1f2a37`, accent `#e4572e`, paper `#ffffff`, muted `#8a94a6`. A second accent is derived by rotating the accent hue about 190 degrees; use it for the second item in any pair. Keep saturation moderate, never neon.

## Type
Unbounded for display (weight 800), Plus Jakarta Sans for labels. Headlines rise word by word out of a mask. Size to fit; headline max about 5 words.

## Motion
Springy, energy about .55, with small overshoot. Tiles pop in staggered on spoken words. Progress lines fill. Counters tick up. Scene progress dots sit at the top.

## Camera
Slow drift by default. Push-in on the number, pan along a process. No shake.

## Sound
Clean pops and soft taps, a low pad at -22 dB. No risers, chimes or high pitched tones.

## Hooks
A promise with a number of steps ("in three steps"), a relatable annoyance ("your week feels messy"), or a "here is how I do it" opener.
