# Vox playbook

**Voice.** A calm, certain narrator (ElevenLabs, speed 1.0 to 1.1). Plain words, one fact a sentence, no hype. Ask one question early, answer it by the end.

**Picking the topic.** Something with a place, a date and a number: a standard that was invented, a border that moved, a price that changed. Check the facts against Wikipedia or a primary source, and quote only what you have read.

**Cutting.** The reference cuts every 4.5 s; aim for 3 to 4 s. Two to four shots per scene, always cut on a spoken word, and never let one picture sit a whole scene. Draw an `ink` arrow, circle or underline on the print the voice is pointing at.

**Maps.** Dive from far to near across the line. Label only what the voice names. Keep labels at least 250 px from the frame edge in 9:16 (use `callout` near the right edge).

**Docs.** Keep to one or two paragraphs, and mark at most two phrases. A 9:16 frame fits only a small camera push (z up to 1.2).

**Pictures.** Archive prints with a sepia `tint` look best. Never use a picture you cannot show a public-domain or CC0 licence for.

**Ending.** The last scene is a `title` card with the topic, then the voice stops. No call to action beyond the community link on the pinned comment.

**Gates.** `megaedit check`, `voice`, a `still` of every scene, then `render --preset 9x16-1080`.
